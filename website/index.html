FAQ

Questions answered

How do I pay? +

Send payment to JazzCash 0331-862-7018, then enter the transaction reference number in the subscription form. Your account is activated within 2 hours of payment verification.

How fast will I receive an alert? +

Pro subscribers receive alerts within 1 minute of a slot opening. VIP subscribers get alerts within 30 seconds. VFS slots typically disappear in 30–90 seconds, so keep your VFS login ready.

Which countries and cities are covered? +

We monitor UK, Italy, France, Germany, Canada, Australia, and Netherlands — all countries processed through VFS Global Pakistan. Cities covered: Lahore, Islamabad, and Karachi.

Can I subscribe for multiple routes? +

Yes. Pro and VIP subscribers can monitor all 7 routes simultaneously. The free trial covers UK only.

How do I cancel or pause my subscription? +

Reply STOP to any WhatsApp alert message and your subscription pauses immediately. Reply START to re-activate, or contact support via WhatsApp.
